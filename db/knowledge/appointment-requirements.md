# Appointment Requirements

Synthetic sample content for CamiConnect development — not real hospital information.

## Booking an appointment

Appointments are requested through the CamiConnect assistant. A request is not final until the doctor confirms it. You will see the status of every request under My Appointments.

Each consultation slot lasts 30 minutes. Please book one slot per concern so the doctor has enough time.

## Before your visit

Arrive at least 15 minutes before your scheduled time to complete registration at the outpatient front desk.

If this is your first visit, registration takes longer because a patient record is created. Arrive 30 minutes early for a first visit.

## Late arrivals and missed appointments

If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, the clinic may ask you to wait for the next open slot or to book again.

An appointment you do not attend and did not cancel is recorded as a no-show.

## Cancelling

You can cancel a requested or confirmed appointment from My Appointments in the assistant. Please cancel as early as possible so the slot can be offered to another patient.

## Minors and dependents

Patients under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian, who should bring their own valid ID.
