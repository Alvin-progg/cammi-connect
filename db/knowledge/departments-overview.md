# Departments Overview

Synthetic sample content for CamiConnect development — not real hospital information.

## How departments are organised

Outpatient care is organised into clinical departments, and each department groups related specialties. The current list of departments, specialties, and doctors comes from the hospital directory; ask the assistant "Which departments do you have?" to see it.

## Choosing where to book

If you know the specialty you need, book with that specialty. If you are unsure, start with a general consultation; the doctor can refer you to a specialist.

The assistant cannot recommend a department or specialty for your symptoms. Please speak with a doctor or nurse for medical advice.

## Referrals

Some specialists see patients only with a referral from another doctor. If you were referred, bring the referral letter to your appointment.
