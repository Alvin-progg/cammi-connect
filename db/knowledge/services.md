# Hospital Services

Synthetic sample content for CamiConnect development — not real hospital information.

## Outpatient consultations

Specialist consultations are available by appointment. Ask the assistant which departments and specialties are available, then book a slot with a doctor.

## Laboratory

The laboratory performs routine blood tests, urinalysis, and other common tests. A doctor's request form is required. Some blood tests need fasting for 8 to 10 hours; the laboratory staff will tell you if your test does.

Laboratory hours are Monday to Saturday, 6:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Results for routine tests are usually released within one working day.

## Imaging

Imaging services include X-ray and ultrasound. A doctor's request form is required, and some ultrasound exams need preparation, such as a full bladder. Follow the instructions given when you schedule the test.

## Pharmacy

The hospital pharmacy is open daily from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Bring your prescription. Prescription medicines are only dispensed with a valid prescription.

## Emergency room

The emergency room is open 24 hours a day, every day. It does not need an appointment.
