# Visit Guidelines

Synthetic sample content for CamiConnect development — not real hospital information.

## Outpatient clinic hours

The outpatient department is open Monday to Saturday from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Manila time. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays. Individual doctors keep their own consultation hours within this window; the assistant shows only the slots that are open.

## Visiting hours for admitted patients

Visiting hours for the wards are from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily. Each patient may have up to two visitors at a time. Children under 12 may not visit the wards.

## What to expect on the day

Check in at the outpatient front desk, then wait to be called at the clinic. A nurse takes your vital signs before the consultation.

## Payment

The cashier accepts cash, debit and credit cards, and major e-wallets. Pay consultation fees at the cashier after your consultation.

## Health and safety

Wear a face mask in the hospital if you have a cough, cold, or fever. Hand sanitizer stations are at every entrance.

## Emergencies

The outpatient clinic does not handle emergencies. For an emergency, go directly to the emergency room, which is open 24 hours, or call 911.
