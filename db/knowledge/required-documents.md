# Required Documents

Synthetic sample content for CamiConnect development — not real hospital information.

## Identification

Bring one valid government-issued photo ID, such as a passport, driver's license, UMID, or national ID. A school ID is accepted for students without a government ID.

## Health insurance and benefits

If you will use PhilHealth or an HMO, bring your member ID card and any letter of authorization (LOA) your HMO requires. Request the LOA from your HMO before the visit; the clinic cannot issue it for you.

Senior citizens and persons with disability should bring their senior citizen or PWD ID to apply the discount.

## Medical records

Bring previous laboratory results, imaging reports, and a list of the medicines you currently take, including the dose. If you were referred by another doctor, bring the referral letter.

## For children

Bring the child's birth certificate or health record book (immunization card) together with the parent's or guardian's valid ID.
