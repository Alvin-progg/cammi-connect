# Payments, Discounts, and Coverage

Synthetic sample content for CamiConnect development — not real hospital information.

## Paying for an appointment booked in the chat

Appointments and tests booked through Cami are paid before the front desk confirms them. After you confirm your booking, Cami shows the amount to pay, a QR code, a reference code, and a deadline. Scan the QR code with GCash, Maya, or your bank app, type the amount, and put the reference code in the message or notes field. Then send a clear photo or screenshot of your receipt in the chat.

The front desk checks that the payment reached the hospital's account. When it does, your appointment moves on to confirmation. If the payment can't be matched, Cami tells you why and you can send the correct receipt.

If no payment is received before the deadline, the request is cancelled and the time slot is released. You can book again anytime.

Please keep your receipt. The hospital cashier issues the official receipt when you arrive.

## Senior citizen and PWD discounts

Senior citizens (60 years old and above) and persons with disability get a 20% discount on consultations, laboratory tests, and imaging, as required by law. Only one of the two discounts can be used per booking.

Choose the discount when Cami asks, and bring your Senior Citizen ID (OSCA ID) or PWD ID on the day of your visit. The front desk checks the ID at check-in. If the ID can't be verified, the discount is removed and the difference is paid at the counter.

## PhilHealth

PhilHealth does not cover most regular outpatient consultations. Cami offers PhilHealth only for services where a PhilHealth benefit applies. Bring your PhilHealth ID or Member Data Record (MDR) on the day of your visit.

## HMO

If you have an HMO card from an accredited HMO, choose "My HMO" when Cami asks how you will pay. The front desk checks your coverage with your HMO. Request a Letter of Authorization (LOA) through your HMO's app or hotline, and bring your HMO card and a valid ID on the day of your visit. If your HMO doesn't approve the visit, Cami will ask you to pay so you can keep your slot.

## Cancellations and refunds

Payments are non-refundable. Once the front desk has verified your payment, the amount is not refunded if you cancel the appointment, whether you cancel it yourself or the hospital cancels it.

## Emergencies

The emergency room is open 24 hours a day and never requires payment before treatment. If you or someone with you is having an emergency, go to the nearest emergency room or call 911.
